# InvoiceCraftly

> InvoiceCraftly is a free invoicing product for creating professional invoices, quotes, estimates, receipts, and credit notes. Core document creation and export work without an account, with custom branding, payment QR options, reusable workspace features, and PDF, PNG, SVG, and CSV exports.

## Developer API

- [Developer Document API](https://invoicecraftly.com/developers/): Beta API for generating invoice PDFs, checking EN16931-core readiness, and returning EN16931-core UBL/XML with bearer API-key authentication.
- [OpenAPI 3.1 specification](https://invoicecraftly.com/developers/openapi.json): Machine-readable contract for the three released v1 API endpoints.
- [APIs.json descriptor](https://invoicecraftly.com/apis.json): Discovery metadata linking the API, documentation, OpenAPI contract, Postman collection, package registries, and public SDK source repositories.
- [Published Postman documentation](https://documenter.getpostman.com/view/2986090/2sBYB1PoQR): Public request examples and endpoint documentation.
- [TypeScript SDK](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@invoicecraftly/client): npm client for the Developer Document API.
- [Python SDK](https://pypi.org/project/invoicecraftly/): Python client for the Developer Document API.

The structured API surface targets EN16931-core only. It does not produce a Peppol BIS artifact, transmit through a Peppol Access Point, or make an official conformance claim.

## Company lookup for invoicing

- [Company Lookup](https://invoicecraftly.com/tools/company-lookup/): Find public company details and start an invoice with reviewed customer information already filled in. Current markets are Norway, Finland, Denmark, and Sweden.
- [Bedriftssøk Norge](https://invoicecraftly.com/no/verktoy/bedriftssok/): Search Brønnøysundregistrenes Enhetsregister by business name or organisation number, review the selected company, then start a Norwegian invoice with customer details filled in.
- [Yrityshaku Suomi](https://invoicecraftly.com/fi/tyokalut/yrityshaku/): Search PRH/YTJ public company data by name or Y-tunnus, review the result, then start a Finnish invoice with selected customer details.
- [CVR-opslag Danmark](https://invoicecraftly.com/dk/vaerktojer/cvr-opslag/): Enter a Danish CVR number, review public company identity and address information, then start a Danish invoice with selected customer details.
- [Företagssök Sverige](https://invoicecraftly.com/se/verktyg/foretagssok/): Search Swedish company information, review the selected organisation, then start a Swedish invoice with customer details filled in.

Company lookup helps with public business details; the issuer should still confirm the customer and transaction-specific tax details before sending an invoice.

## Tools

- [SEPA payment QR code generator](https://invoicecraftly.com/tools/sepa-payment-qr-generator): Creates an EPC/SEPA payment QR code from reviewed EUR bank-transfer details for a compatible banking app to prefill; it does not authorize, submit, settle, or confirm a transfer.
- [IBAN validator](https://invoicecraftly.com/tools/iban-validator): Checks whether an IBAN has a valid basic format and checksum; it does not contact a bank or confirm account ownership.
- [Static QR code generator](https://invoicecraftly.com/tools/qr-code-generator): Creates static QR codes for reviewed links, contact cards, email, phone numbers, and plain text.
- [Invoice readiness checklist generator](https://invoicecraftly.com/tools/invoice-readiness-checklist): Builds a document-aware pre-send review checklist.
- [Invoice due date calculator](https://invoicecraftly.com/tools/invoice-due-date-calculator): Adds an agreed calendar-day payment term to an invoice issue date.
- [Invoice number generator and format planner](https://invoicecraftly.com/tools/invoice-number-generator): Previews a prefix, optional year, safe separator, and padded invoice sequence.
- [EU VAT number checker (VIES)](https://invoicecraftly.com/tools/vat-number-checker): Checks one EU Member State or Northern Ireland VAT number against the European Commission VIES service.
- [VAT calculator](https://invoicecraftly.com/tools/vat-calculator): Adds VAT to a net amount or extracts VAT from a gross amount using a rate the user supplies.
- [E-invoice XML viewer & checker](https://invoicecraftly.com/tools/e-invoice-xml-viewer): Opens supported Peppol BIS Billing 3, UBL, OIOUBL and Finvoice XML, shows mapped invoice details, and runs InvoiceCraftly’s bounded deterministic preflight. Formal EN 16931 or Peppol business-rule validation, recipient capability and network acceptance are shown separately when those checks were not run.

## Document types

- [Invoice](https://invoicecraftly.com/editor/?type=invoice): Request payment for supplied or agreed goods or services.
- [Quote](https://invoicecraftly.com/document-types/quote): Present proposed pricing before acceptance.
- [Estimate](https://invoicecraftly.com/document-types/estimate): Present an informed approximation before final scope or cost is known.
- [Receipt](https://invoicecraftly.com/document-types/receipt): Record payment already received.
- [Credit note](https://invoicecraftly.com/document-types/credit-note): Reduce or correct an earlier invoice.

## Workspace and storage

Core document creation does not require an account. Working documents are saved on the current device by default. Users can export workspace backups, and eligible signed-in users can choose supported Personal Cloud backup options such as Dropbox or Google Drive.

- [Account](https://invoicecraftly.com/account/): Sign-in, account controls, and available Personal Cloud options.
- [Privacy](https://invoicecraftly.com/privacy): Detailed information about document storage, optional services, account data, analytics choices, and legal responsibility.
- [Security](https://invoicecraftly.com/security): Security, recoverability, network-service, and product-limit details.

## Key product limits

- InvoiceCraftly helps prepare invoices and related business documents; tax treatment, legal requirements, record retention, and recipient-specific e-invoicing rules depend on the transaction and jurisdiction.
- Public company lookup can fill customer details, but it is not a substitute for checking the customer and transaction-specific VAT or tax treatment.
- A payment QR carries payment instructions; confirm the encoded destination and payment details before sending the document.
- EN 16931 / UBL XML export is available in beta; Peppol, EHF, or other network delivery remains a separate step.
- Template, use-case, and screenshot data is fictional unless explicitly stated otherwise.
